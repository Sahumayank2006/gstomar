  1. 01

    Step 1: Open the voting site.

    Go to voting.vtools.ieee.org ↗ (opens in a new tab), or use the link in the ballot email from IEEE vTools Voting.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Sign in.

    Use your IEEE account email and password. If you have forgotten it, use Forgot password on the IEEE sign-in page.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Open your ballot.

    Under Ballots, find IEEE India Council Chair-Elect 2027 and click Click Here To Vote.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Choose Prof. Geetam Singh Tomar.

    Tick his name in the candidate list. You can read every candidate’s statement on the ballot.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Click “Complete Ballot”.

    On the Confirm Vote page, click Complete Ballot. Your vote is not recorded until you do this.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Check “Vote completed”.

    Then please remind one colleague in your Section.

Please note. Vote only at the official address. The ballot email goes to the address registered with your IEEE membership. If no ballot reaches you, contact the IEEE India Council Nomination Committee through ieeeindiacouncil.org ↗ (opens in a new tab). This site never asks for your IEEE credentials.

About

Air Force officer, academic, researcher and IEEE volunteer.

I served 17 years in the Indian Air Force and have spent 25 years in academia. I have led institutions as Director of REC Sonbhadra and of THDC IHEIT, Govt. of Uttarakhand, and today I am Director of Machine Intelligence Research Labs (MIR Labs), Gwalior.

Research remains at the centre of my work: more than 240 publications, 14 books, published and granted patents, and 14 Ph.D. scholars guided.

In IEEE, I served as Chair of the Madhya Pradesh Subsection. Since 2021 I have been Chairperson of the IEEE Madhya Pradesh Section, and I serve on the Executive Committee of the IEEE India Council.

“As Chair-Elect, I want to bring that experience to work for every member and every Section across India.”

Prof. Geetam Singh Tomar · Senior Member, IEEE
